A payouts tool on Supabase, with the decisions that matter moved somewhere a leaked secret key cannot reach. Each claim below is live on this page, not described.

Bank details are ciphertextEncrypted in the Tide enclave before they leave your browser. Supabase stores bytes it has no key for.
Reading them takes a quorumThe network decrypts only for vault-reader, a role administrators grant by approving in their own enclaves.
A payout is a signatureFourteen of twenty independent nodes sign the instruction, and refuse anything over the limit. Edit a row and it stops verifying.
